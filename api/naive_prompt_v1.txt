   (e.g., fasting/food requirements for oral PCSK9 inhibitors vs. Leqvio's
   biannual physician-administered profile; disability progression misses
   by competitor BTK inhibitors like fenebrutinib vs. remibrutinib).
2. CLINICAL TRIAL REGISTRY TRACKING: Analysts monitor ClinicalTrials.gov for
   sample-size upsizing, primary completion date shifts (e.g., Abelacimab
   slipping from 2027 to 2028), and endpoint hierarchy alterations.
3. PATENT LITIGATION, EXCLUSIVITIES & SETTLEMENTS: Analysts track District Court
   decisions (e.g., Delaware court rulings on generic radioligand therapies
   like Lutathera), Hatch-Waxman settlements, and exact Loss of Exclusivity (LOE)
   dates post-pediatric exclusivity (e.g., Kisqali Q3 2031 vs 2032).
4. ACCELERATED APPROVAL PRECEDENTS: Analysts challenge management on whether
   Phase II biomarker data (e.g., Votoplam in Huntington's, Del-brax in FSHD)
   can support early accelerated filing vs. requiring full Phase III studies.

Intelligence section and live market data.
